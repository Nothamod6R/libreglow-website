# GlowSnap

GlowSnap is a modern open-source screenshot and visual editing application built specifically for Linux.

Designed for creators, developers, testers, technical writers, educators, and everyday Linux users, GlowSnap combines fast screen capture, a beautiful editing experience, and a native Linux workflow into a single application.

Rather than being "just another screenshot tool," GlowSnap focuses on helping users create polished visuals without unnecessary complexity or cloud services.

---

# Overview

Most screenshot applications stop after capturing the screen.

GlowSnap continues the workflow.

Capture your screen.

Annotate it.

Organize it.

Export it.

Share it.

All inside one application.

The goal is to make screenshots feel like a complete creative workflow instead of a simple operating system utility.

---

# Philosophy

GlowSnap follows several core principles.

## Linux First

GlowSnap is built specifically for Linux.

Instead of treating Linux as a secondary platform, the project embraces native Linux technologies and desktop integration from the beginning.

---

## Open Source

Every part of the project is open-source.

Users can inspect the code, contribute improvements, suggest features, or build their own versions.

Transparency is one of the project's core values.

---

## Privacy First

GlowSnap does not rely on cloud services.

Your screenshots remain on your computer.

No account.

No tracking.

No analytics.

No uploads.

Your data belongs to you.

---

## Beautiful by Default

Screenshots are often shared with other people.

GlowSnap focuses on making screenshots look clean and professional with minimal effort.

Instead of requiring multiple editing applications, GlowSnap provides the essential tools directly after capture.

---

# Why GlowSnap?

Linux has many excellent screenshot utilities.

GlowSnap does not try to replace them simply by adding another capture button.

Instead, it focuses on the complete experience after the screenshot is taken.

It combines capture, editing, organization, and presentation inside a single workflow.

Whether you're documenting software, creating tutorials, writing documentation, reporting bugs, or publishing content online, GlowSnap helps transform raw screenshots into polished assets.

---

# Features

## Screenshot Studio

Capture screenshots quickly using multiple modes.

Current and planned capture options include:

- Full screen capture
- Area selection capture
- Multi-monitor support
- Screenshot gallery
- Full-screen image viewer
- Fast preview after capture

The screenshot gallery makes it easy to browse previous captures without searching through folders.

---

## Visual Editor

GlowSnap includes a built-in editor designed specifically for screenshot annotation.

Available tools include:

- Free drawing
- Arrows
- Lines
- Rectangles
- Circles
- Number annotations
- Text editing
- Crop tool
- Custom colors
- Opacity controls

The editor is designed to remain lightweight while providing everything needed for technical documentation and tutorials.

---

## Screenshot Gallery

Every screenshot is organized automatically.

Instead of manually browsing filesystem folders, GlowSnap provides an integrated gallery where screenshots can be viewed, searched, and reopened for editing.

Future versions may include:

- Favorites
- Tags
- Search
- Collections
- Smart folders

---

## Recording

Experimental screen recording support is currently under development.

Future recording capabilities may include:

- Full screen recording
- Area recording
- Window recording
- Audio recording
- Microphone support
- System audio
- GIF export
